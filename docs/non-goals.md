# Non-Goals

- No spyware, stalkerware, hidden monitoring, keylogging, secret audio/video recording, coercive location tracking, or silent partner surveillance.
- No good spouse, good parent, obedience, truthfulness, trustworthiness, human value, who-was-right, credit, employment, insurance, housing, education, relationship, or eligibility score.
- No diagnosis of mental illness from passive or behavioral data.
- No disclosure of one member's inferred mood, stress, or private signal to another member without explicit owner authorization.
- No automatic public posts, spending, shared-data mutation, calendar changes, applications, or messages without explicit approval.
- No real external connectors that require secrets until the integration framework, credentials, and review process are ready.
- No claim of million-user readiness, HIPAA compliance, medical validity, legal compliance, or complete phone-data access.
