# Insight Engine

## Infrastructure Boundary

An Insight is an `ai_insight` entity plus a typed extension containing title, description, reasoning, evidence, confidence, generation method, lifecycle status, and optional follow-up actions. The historical type name leaves room for reviewed AI output, but the contract also supports human-authored and deterministic insights.

No insight is generated by this milestone. There are no model calls, embeddings, observation writes, background calculations, prompts, or AI summaries.

## Evidence Model

Insight evidence is represented by relationships to canonical entities. Every evidence read remains actor-scoped under RLS. An insight does not copy inaccessible content, and its existence must not reveal counts, categories, relationship topology, or other facts about another adult's private records.

Reasoning must distinguish:

- Recorded or self-asserted evidence.
- Deterministic calculations.
- Statistical association.
- Future model inference.
- Recommendation or professional judgment.

Confidence is bounded and source-specific. It is not certainty, a person score, or proof of real-world completeness.

## Lifecycle

Candidate insights may become accepted, dismissed, or expired. Status records user handling, not objective truth. Corrections create a new base-entity version. Deleted state removes the insight from non-owner access while preserving controlled history until retention processing.

## Privacy And Safety

Insight entities cannot be household-visible under the current constraints. They are private by default; explicit sharing cannot expose hidden evidence or version history. A future engine may only calculate over rows already readable by the transaction-scoped actor.

Prohibited outputs include diagnosis, mental-state claims about another adult, relationship adjudication, person ranking, universal scores, and employment, credit, insurance, housing, education, medical, legal, or safety-critical decisions. Incomplete data must never be described as complete real-world evidence.

## Future Execution Contract

Before an insight generator launches it must define input types, purpose, formula/model version, minimum evidence, missing-data semantics, uncertainty, owner, visibility, expiration, correction, explanation, provider data flow, logging, retention, and disable controls. Deterministic and model-generated paths remain distinguishable in storage and UI.
